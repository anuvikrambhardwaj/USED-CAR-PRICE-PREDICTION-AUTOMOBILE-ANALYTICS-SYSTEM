Section 1Project Overview & Problem Statement

The objective of this project is to develop an automated Used Car Price Prediction & Automobile Analytics System using fundamental Data Mining and Machine Learning techniques. In secondary automobile markets, vehicle depreciation is non-linear and heavily influenced by an interplay of mechanical specifications (horsepower, engine capacity), temporal factors (manufacturing year/age), usage wear (odometer mileage), and brand reputation.

Section 2Data Mining Mathematical Formulations

1. Interquartile Range (IQR) Outlier Detection & Winsorization

Outliers represent extreme variance or data entry errors. The Interquartile Range (IQR) measures statistical dispersion between the 75th percentile ($Q_3$) and 25th percentile ($Q_1$):

IQR = Q3 - Q1
Lower Bound = max(0, Q1 - 1.5 * IQR)
Upper Bound = Q3 + 1.5 * IQR
Capping Rule: x_treated = min(max(x, Lower Bound), Upper Bound)

Why Winsorization / Capping? Rather than dropping outlier rows (which permanently discards training information), clipping values to the outer bounds preserves sample volume while removing skew.

2. StandardScaler Feature Normalization (Z-Score)

Ensures that features with large numerical magnitudes (e.g., Mileage up to 200,000) do not dominate features with small numerical ranges (e.g., Engine Size 1.0 to 5.0):

z = (x - μ) / σ
where μ = Mean, σ = Standard Deviation
3. Evaluation Metrics
R² = 1 - (∑ (y_i - ŷ_i)² / ∑ (y_i - ȳ)²)
RMSE = √( (1/n) * ∑ (y_i - ŷ_i)² )
MAE = (1/n) * ∑ |y_i - ŷ_i|

Section 4Viva-Voce / Lab Defense Q&A Guide

Q1: Why use Median rather than Mean for missing mileage and engine size?
Mean is highly sensitive to extreme outliers and asymmetric skewness. In automotive datasets, mileage is right-skewed; median represents the robust 50th percentile and prevents introducing synthetic skew into imputed records.
Q2: Why use IQR Outlier Capping (Winsorization) over dropping rows?
Dropping rows permanently shrinks the training set and can introduce selection bias. Capping clips extreme anomalies to $[Q1 - 1.5 \times IQR, Q3 + 1.5 \times IQR]$, preserving the observation and its valid attributes while bounding leverage on the regression line.
Q3: What is the purpose of One-Hot Encoding in this pipeline?
Categorical variables like `brand` (Toyota, BMW), `fuel_type` (Petrol, Diesel), and `transmission` (Automatic, Manual) have no natural mathematical ordinal ranking. One-hot encoding converts them into binary indicator columns (0 or 1), preventing false ordinal assumptions in regression matrices.
Q4: How does R² differ from RMSE?
$R^2$ is a scale-independent metric between 0 and 1 indicating what fraction of target variance is explained by features. RMSE is scale-dependent and measures average prediction error directly in target units (dollars, $), heavily penalizing large outliers.
